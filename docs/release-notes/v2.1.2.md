# TomiLite v2.1.2

**Since v2.1.1** — Meetings: record → local transcription → AI minutes → action items → tasks. Plus a table of contents for every note.

## ✨ Features

- **🎙 Meeting Intelligence** — a new **Meeting** panel that closes the loop from a conversation to work on the board. Record a meeting (microphone + system audio mixed into one track), transcribe it **locally** with a bundled [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) binary, then let AI produce the summary, decisions and action items — and turn any action item into a task with one click.
  - **The audio never leaves your machine.** Only the transcript text is sent to the LLM you configured, and only when you ask for a summary. Speech-to-text runs entirely offline; no ffmpeg is bundled and no audio is ever uploaded.
  - **Long meetings cost what short ones do.** The AI stage is map-reduce: cheap model per transcript chunk, expensive model only over the chunk summaries. A three-hour meeting costs roughly the same at the synthesis step as a twenty-minute one.
  - **A silent recording is treated as an emergency, not a detail.** If system audio sits below −55 dBFS for 30 seconds while the microphone has signal, a banner warns you that the remote side may not be captured — Windows loopback returning a valid-but-dead stream is common, and finding out after the meeting is unacceptable.
  - **Honest speakers.** No diarization is performed, so the transcript shows `Speaker 1` / `Speaker 2` — never an invented name. AI role guesses are shown as guesses.
  - Minutes email goes out through your own SMTP, with the transcript optionally attached. Action items become tasks (`TL-N`) that you can jump straight to.
  - Model downloads (tiny / base / small / medium) happen on demand from Settings → Meetings; **base** is the default.
  - A first-run consent gate explains that both the microphone and system audio are written to a local file, and that recording may require the consent of every participant depending on where you are. **This is not legal advice.**
- **📑 Note outline (目次)** — every note now gets a table of contents alongside the editor, generated from its headings. Click any entry to jump to that section; the entry you're currently reading is highlighted as you scroll. Deeper headings indent, so a long structured note stays readable at a glance.

## 🔒 Privacy

TomiLite stays local-first — tasks, notes, emails, reports, chats, meeting recordings and transcripts, API keys and git data live in your own SQLite database under `~/.tomilite/`.

Meetings get their own disclosure in **Settings → Meetings**, split into three separate lines rather than one slogan: **audio** is processed and stored locally and never uploaded, the **transcript** is stored locally, and the **AI summary** is the one step where transcript text is sent to your LLM provider (or through the TomiVector gateway on a hosted trial).

## 🐛 Fixes

- **`shell.openExternal` no longer crashes** — the Electron main process destructured `shell` without importing it in that scope. It now goes through a proper IPC handler.
